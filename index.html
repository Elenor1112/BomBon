The Editor's Letter

Your little escape, made for women.

Behind an unassuming door on El-Safa Street, Sheraton, the noise of Cairo softens into candlelight, warm stone and the hush of steam. Bombòn is a spa for ladies only. It's a place to take off the day, wrap up in an olive robe and let someone else take care of you for once.

Our therapists blend time-honoured rituals with modern skincare: Moroccan black soap and mud, the Turkish hammam, the Japanese head spa, golden facials and slow aromatic massage. Come for an hour, or stay for a whole pamper day.

With love, the Bombòn team
